# IAIRS landing experience

Local development: `npm run dev -- --port 5173 --strictPort`. Open http://localhost:5173/. Chapter two is http://localhost:5173/#system. Publishing and Git commits remain manual.

`storyboard-director.js` owns the scroll state, GSAP timeline, Lenis clock, typography, evidence controls, accessibility and console handoff. The existing routes and console remain available immediately.

`singularity-world.js` owns one persistent Three.js scene. `singularity-portal.js` integrates bent light paths and samples a rotating procedural accretion field. The warm charcoal opening uses ivory type and vermilion accents. The portal uses no bitmap, video, or generated image. `singularity-lanes.js` morphs the converging channels into the evidence network and containment boundary. `singularity-membrane.js` carries the transition surface. `singularity-analysis.js` renders the textured threat globe and the risk instrument; its four sectors encode the actual 40/30/20/10 maximum contributions. `singularity-particles.js` moves stable particle identities between typography, signals, the calculated score and actual console panel bounds.

Chapter two retains the user-approved centered, two-line composition. Its surfaces use the CC0 Poly Haven Studio Small 03 environment for reflections. Surface geometry, travelling highlights, particles, lensing and atmosphere run live. Pixel density adapts to rendering cost. Hidden tabs pause, reduced motion holds stable poses, and the landing renderer is disposed when the visitor navigates into the actual console.

Risk values come from the existing illustrative SSH rule engine through `journey-data.js`. Exploring the landing does not create incidents or alter the local sample dataset. The embedded console is the actual application rendering a transient preview of the same evaluated events; it does not run a second 3D renderer. Investigate this example explicitly records those events locally and opens that case in the console. Open your workspace opens the existing workspace without recording the example.

References: [Lusion's DOM/WebGL scroll synchronization](https://webgl-scroll-sync.lusion.co/), [Eric Bruneton's black hole rendering research](https://ebruneton.github.io/black_hole_shader/), and [Poly Haven Studio Small 03](https://polyhaven.com/a/studio_small_03). The portal implementation is project-native GLSL. Unused experimental files are retained under ignored `test-results/retired-world-20261024/` and are excluded from the production assets.


## Opening sequence and visitor controls

The initial HTML paints the loading screen before the main JavaScript arrives. A small independent WebGL renderer samples the actual IAIRS letterforms into five surfaces. The glyphs shed signal points and fold into reflective silver ribbons with vermilion backs. The sculpture responds to the pointer, then compresses into a blade as a diagonal mask splits the screen open into the live hero. This renderer uses procedural studio reflections and local fonts, requires no images or Three.js downloads, and releases its GPU resources after entry. A canvas typography version covers browsers without WebGL. Progress is based on completed preparation tasks: scene module, four local font families, Earth texture, studio HDR environment, the actual console preview, and Three.js asynchronous shader compilation. The preview loads eagerly so the final handoff does not wait for its first render. The rolling three-digit counter follows real weighted completion and each of six preparation markers corresponds to an actual task. There is no timer-driven fake percentage. Skip intro and Open workspace are available throughout; slow loading surfaces an entry hint after 12 seconds. The loader honors reduced motion and the saved motion preference, and pauses its renderer when the tab is hidden. The covered hero precompiles its shaders without continuously drawing behind the loader; drawing resumes as the diagonal reveal begins. It appears on an initial landing load, not direct console links, embedded previews, or ordinary chapter navigation.

The guided tour has persistent Pause, Resume and Exit controls. Scrolling pauses it, and chapter navigation exits it. Resuming starts from the visitor's current position. Globe signals show their evidence cards only while hovered or keyboard-focused. A mouse click does not pin the card. On touch devices, tap a signal to inspect it and tap outside to close. The globe remains unobstructed at rest. The risk instrument labels the calculated severity and simulated response beside the score; frequency contributions explicitly explain their +30 cap. Factor explanations remain selected when evidence changes. Open this case explicitly saves the illustrative case; Enter the console opens the existing dataset.

A viewport resize preserves story progress and remeasures the actual console handoff targets. All changes remain local and uncommitted.

## Editorial interface

The presentation layer now uses Cormorant Garamond for statements, scores and major evidence counts; its actual italic face supplies the gold secondary lines. Inter handles readable interface text, with IBM Plex Mono reserved for indices and identifiers. The Latin italic face is hosted locally as a 23,660-byte WOFF2 from the official Google Fonts distribution, under the existing Cormorant SIL OFL license. No studio fonts, artwork or code were copied.

The detection chapter has one dominant observed count, actual elapsed time and an 80-mark ruler whose filled marks correspond to the example events. Its threshold remains 20 failures within 60 seconds. The assessment uses a transparent four-row ledger, real contributions, a fine custom range control, concise contextual notes and a reasoning navigation strip. The score's particle targets are sampled using its actual computed font family and weight. The desktop instrument is slightly smaller and shifted left to keep it clear of the ledger.

Text and actions in the final chapter are withheld until the outgoing assessment text has cleared. The physical monitor, portal, animated backgrounds, approved centered second chapter and hover-only evidence behavior are retained. The preview receives typography and surface treatments without changing the existing standalone workspace.
